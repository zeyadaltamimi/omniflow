# OmniFlow brain — house rules
# This file is read by the tool on every run. Edit it on GitHub to change how ideas are created (no code needed).
# Lines starting with # are notes for you; everything else is sent to Gemini.

Market: Saudi Arabia and the Gulf first. Settings that feel local and premium: modern Riyadh interiors, Najdi majlis, desert at golden hour, Jeddah corniche, Boulevard nights, Diriyah mud-brick, modern cafés.
People: modest, Saudi-appropriate styling (thobe and shemagh, elegant abaya). Natural, confident, never exaggerated acting.

Signature-moment library (pick or remix, never copy literally):
- Perfume / oud: the scent visualised as golden smoke that draws a path through a room; oud chips melting into light; a spray that freezes in mid-air and turns into the ingredients on the label.
- Incense burner (mabkhara): smoke ribbons that wrap the product like silk; a guest's welcome told through smoke drifting from one hand to another.
- Coffee: beans roasting in slow motion with crackle; a pour that becomes a desert dune; steam that draws the café's skyline.
- Sweets / donuts / cakes: the glaze as a slow waterfall; a bite in extreme close-up with crumbs falling like sand; layers revealed by a clean cut.
- Juice / milk: fruit bursting into the liquid; a milk crown that becomes the bottle's shape; condensation racing down the glass.
- Jewelry / watches / prayer beads: light travelling across each stone or bead; the camera gliding along the piece while it is worn; reflections that reveal the setting.
- Skincare / pharmacy: the active ingredient shown as clean texture (gel, foam, droplets); calm comfort and confidence as the result; never medical before/after.
- Fashion: fabric reacting to wind and light; a confident walk with a match-cut between looks.
- Real estate: rising drone reveal of the exact property; top-down hold with space for area and price; dusk transition with lights turning on; never add or change buildings.
- Restaurants: flame and sizzle macro; the signature dish landing on the table; guests' first-bite reaction.
- Gyms: lights switching on row by row; chalk dust in slow motion; the athlete's confident stillness.

Banned clichés: random sparkles, product floating in an empty void without meaning, generic "happy people smiling at the camera", lens flares everywhere, text rendered by the AI.

Every ad must have: a hook in the first 2 seconds, one memorable signature moment, the real product clearly visible and accurate, and an ending on the product.

Motion choreography toolkit (use inside every clip):
- Camera paths that evolve: macro push-in → orbit → rising crane; low tracking → whip-pan reveal; top-down → tilt-up to hero.
- In-shot transitions: match-move on a shape (a drop becomes a sun, a ring becomes a coffee cup rim), whip-through, focus pull from foreground to product, light sweep reveal, smoke or fabric wipe.
- Speed ramps: slow motion on the key physical moment, back to real time for the reveal.
- The world reacts to the product: liquid crowns, steam rises on cue, fabric unfurls, sand shifts, lights switch on in rhythm.
- Sound lands exactly on the final hero frame (sound effects only when the user's audio setting includes them).
- Never a lone slow drift with nothing happening.

Product images (image mode):
- Main store image: pure white background, product sharp and centered, nothing else — it must pass Salla/Noon/Amazon rules.
- Every other image in a campaign shares one palette taken from the pack and one hero prop from the product's real ingredients or use.
- Banners are Arabic right-to-left: product on the left, clean calm space on the right for the headline.
- Occasions to suggest when they fit: Ramadan, Eid, Saudi National Day (green and white, tasteful), Founding Day, White Friday.
